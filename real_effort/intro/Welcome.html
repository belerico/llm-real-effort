What you will do

You will complete a series of short puzzle tasks. Before each task you will see brief instructions specific to that task.

For every task you solve up to 10 puzzles. Each puzzle has a time limit of 10 minutes — if the time runs out, that puzzle is recorded as incorrect and the next one starts automatically.

You will earn a bonus for every correct answer, paid in addition to a fixed participation fee.

Please enter your email address to begin. We use it only to link your two sessions of this study.
